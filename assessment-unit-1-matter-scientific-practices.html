Unit 1 Assessment — Matter and Scientific Practices

5th Grade Science · Modules 1–8

Part A — Multiple Choice (select the best answer)
Question 1
A scientist observes a shell and writes: 'The shell has two cracks.' A classmate writes: 'The shell must have been dropped.' Which statement is the observation?
A 'The shell has two cracks' — it describes only what can be seen
B 'The shell must have been dropped' — it explains what happened
C Both are observations because both are about the same shell
D Neither is an observation because they don't include measurements
Question 2
When sugar is stirred into water, the total mass of the mixture is 105 grams. Before mixing, the sugar was 5 grams and the water was 100 grams. What does this show?
A Some of the sugar disappeared when it dissolved in the water
B Matter is conserved — the total mass stays the same even after dissolving
C The water gained mass because the sugar added extra particles
D Mixing always changes the mass of the substances
Question 3
A student is testing whether water temperature affects how fast salt dissolves. She uses warm water in one cup and cold water in another. To make this a fair test, what must stay the same?
A The amount of salt and the size of the container
B The temperature of the water in each cup
C The type of container, but the amount of salt can be different
D Only the time she stirs each cup
Question 4
Which best describes the difference between a solid and a liquid?
A A solid has a definite shape; a liquid takes the shape of its container
B A solid is heavier than a liquid made of the same material
C A liquid has particles that stop moving; a solid has fast-moving particles
D A solid can be identified by smell; a liquid can only be identified by color
Part B — Short Response (write in complete sentences)
Question 5
A student mixes iron filings with sand. She then uses a magnet to pull out the iron. Which property of iron is she using to separate the mixture? Explain.
Question 6
You have two white powders. Describe two different tests you could do to tell them apart. Explain what you would observe for each test.
Question 7
A student dissolves 10 grams of salt in 200 grams of water. She weighs the mixture. Predict what the scale will show and explain why using the word 'particles.'
Question 8
What is the difference between an observation and an inference? Give one example of each from a science investigation.
Part C — Claim · Evidence · Reasoning
🔬
CER Question
Does matter disappear when it dissolves or changes form? Use evidence from the unit to support your answer.

Use the sentence starter to help you begin.

C
Claim
Your answer — one clear sentence that directly answers the question.

💡 Start with: "My claim is that matter does / does not disappear when it changes, because…"

E
Evidence
Specific data, observations, or examples from the unit that support your claim.

💡 Start with: "In my investigation, the mass before the change was ___ grams and after was ___ grams, which shows…"

R
Reasoning
Explain the science — why does your evidence support your claim?

💡 Start with: "This supports conservation of matter because the particles…"
