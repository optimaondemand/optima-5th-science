5th Grade Science · Modules 16–19

Part A — Multiple Choice (select the best answer)
Question 1
A book sits still on a table. The table pushes up on the book with 3 newtons of force, and gravity pulls the book down with 3 newtons of force. What is true about the book's motion?
A The book will accelerate upward because the table force wins
B The book stays still because the forces are balanced and equal
C The book will slowly sink because gravity is always stronger
D The book will move sideways because the forces cancel out
Question 2
A willow tree started as a small seed and grew to weigh 164 pounds. The soil it grew in barely lost any mass. Where did most of the tree's mass come from?
A From the nutrients in the soil
B From the water and minerals the roots absorbed
C Mostly from carbon dioxide in the air and water
D From the sunlight that the leaves absorbed
Question 3
In a food web, a plant uses sunlight to make food. A caterpillar eats the plant. A bird eats the caterpillar. Where did the bird's energy originally come from?
A From the caterpillar's muscles
B From the plant's roots
C Originally from the Sun — it was transferred through the food web
D From the air that surrounded the plant
Question 4
Which body system is most responsible for moving oxygen from the air you breathe to your muscles?
A The digestive system, which breaks down food for energy
B The respiratory and circulatory systems working together
C The skeletal system, which supports the organs
D The nervous system, which sends signals to the lungs
Part B — Short Response (write in complete sentences)
Question 5
A student pushes two stacked books from opposite sides with equal force. The books don't move. Explain why using the words 'balanced forces.'
Question 6
Plants need water and air (carbon dioxide) to grow. A classmate says, 'Plants get all their food from soil.' Is this correct? Use evidence from the lesson to explain.
Question 7
Trace the energy from the Sun to a student running a race. Name at least three steps in the energy pathway.
Question 8
Describe one way your respiratory system and circulatory system work together. What would happen to your muscles if one of those systems stopped working?
Part C — Claim · Evidence · Reasoning
🔬
CER Question
Where does a plant get the materials it needs to grow? Use the Van Helmont/willow tree evidence and your lesson knowledge to build your argument.

A guide is provided below. You may use it or write your own opening.

C
Claim
Your answer — one clear sentence that directly answers the question.

💡 Start with: "My claim is that plants get most of their mass from…"

E
Evidence
Specific data, observations, or examples from the unit that support your claim.

💡 Start with: "The evidence from the willow tree experiment showed that the tree gained ___ pounds but the soil only lost…"

R
Reasoning
Explain the science — why does your evidence support your claim?

💡 Start with: "This proves plants do not get most of their mass from soil because…"
