Part A — Multiple Choice (select the best answer)
Question 1
A cactus has a thick waxy stem, shallow but wide-spreading roots, and spines instead of flat leaves. What type of adaptation are these?
A Behavioral adaptations — actions the cactus takes to survive
B Physical adaptations — body structures that help the cactus survive in dry environments
C Learned adaptations — things the cactus figured out from its environment
D Inherited behaviors passed down from other plants
Question 2
A forest fire destroys most of the trees in an area. Which organisms are most likely to survive in the short term?
A Large predators, because they can move away quickly
B Organisms with adaptations to open, sunny, or disturbed environments
C Organisms that depend on the burned trees for food and shelter
D All organisms equally, because fires affect all species the same
Question 3
In an ecosystem, which role do decomposers play in the matter cycle?
A They produce food using sunlight
B They eat producers and pass energy to consumers
C They break down dead organisms and return nutrients to the environment
D They compete with consumers for the same food sources
Question 4
The Milky Way is best described as:
A A single star system with many orbiting planets
B A small group of about 10 to 20 nearby stars
C A galaxy — a system of hundreds of billions of stars, gas, and dust
D A solar system made up of our Sun and eight planets
Part B — Short Response (write in complete sentences)
Question 5
An arctic fox has thick white fur in winter and thinner brown fur in summer. Identify the physical and behavioral adaptations in this description and explain how each one helps the fox survive.
Question 6
A population of mice lives in a snowy field. Most mice are brown, but a few are white. A hawk hunts from the air. Over many generations, what would you predict happens to the population? Explain using the ideas of survival and inherited traits.
Question 7
Describe the role of a decomposer in an ecosystem's matter cycle. What would happen to an ecosystem if all the decomposers disappeared?
Question 8
Why does the Sun look much bigger and brighter than other stars in the night sky, even though many of those stars are actually much larger than our Sun?
